# EDA findings (fixture / synthetic panel)

This note is the feature-engineering contract for `ml/train.py`. Charts live in `eda/figures/` and are regenerated by `python eda/run_eda.py`. The panel is **synthetic and labeled as such** — a stand-in for a joined EDGAR + transcript + returns tape, not live market data.

## What we looked at

1. Historical guidance-beat / miss rates by ticker and quarter.
2. Correlation between earnings-call tone signals (sentiment, hedging-token rate) and a next-session reaction proxy.
3. Missingness, outliers, and unit inconsistencies in XBRL-style fundamentals.
4. Class balance for the beat/miss label.
5. Chunk-length distribution from the filing-aware chunker.

## Findings that drive the model

**Beat rates are sticky but not stationary.** Names like NVDA beat more often in this panel; AAPL is closer to even. The cross-sectional mean also drifts by quarter. That is why `last_4q_beat_rate` is a feature, and why the train/test cut is a **walk-forward split by fiscal quarter** rather than a random row shuffle. Shuffling would leak 2024Q4 information into a model asked to score 2023Q2.

**Tone is a weak but real signal.** Call sentiment correlates positively with the next-session reaction proxy; hedging-token rate is higher on misses. Neither is strong enough to use alone — they sit alongside fundamentals so the tree cannot overfit a single lexicon. Features: `call_sentiment`, `hedge_token_rate`.

**XBRL units are a trap.** Across filers the same concept arrives as USD, USD millions, or USD billions, and share counts as actual vs thousands. We never feed raw levels into the model. Features are scale-free: `revenue_growth_yoy`, `gross_margin_delta`, `ocf_to_ni`, `share_count_change`. Outliers in `revenue_growth_yoy` (NVDA-style) are compressed with `tanh` in the DGP comments and left raw for the tree, which can split on them.

**Guidance width is information.** When management publishes a range, a wider band lines up with more misses in this panel (`guidance_range_width`). A missing range is encoded as 0 and accompanied by the hedge-rate feature rather than imputed.

**Class balance is usable.** Beat rate sits near 55/45. No resampling, no class weights in the baseline. We still report precision and recall, not just accuracy.

**Chunks should not swallow tables.** The chunk-length histogram is bimodal: short narrative passages and slightly longer table blocks. The chunker is forbidden from splitting a table across chunks (`ingestion/chunker.py`). Retrieval features inherit that contract: a cited number always lives in one chunk.

## Feature set (frozen)

`last_4q_beat_rate`, `surprise_lag1`, `revenue_growth_yoy`, `gross_margin_delta`, `call_sentiment`, `hedge_token_rate`, `guidance_range_width`, `ocf_to_ni`, `share_count_change`, `sector_momentum`, `implied_move`, `short_interest_proxy`.

Logistic regression is the baseline; XGBoost + Optuna is the candidate promoted through the MLflow registry. Comparison always includes test AUC against the logistic baseline, not a single number in isolation.
